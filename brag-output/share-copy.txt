Exit signs hang right where the smoke goes first, so we put ours on the floor. SAFE-EXIT runs Dijkstra on an ESP32: when a flame sensor trips, that corridor's weight goes to infinity and the LED path redraws itself toward another exit, no network needed. Group 21, Electrical Engineering UI, now building it at 1:50.
